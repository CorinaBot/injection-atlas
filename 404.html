Educational anatomy tool

This atlas maps common clinical injection routes and landmarks on photoreal male and female surface anatomy. It is not a protocol, not product advice, and not permission to inject.

  • Needle sizes and depths are typical adult ranges. Body composition changes everything.
  • Peptide, exosome, and stem-cell filters are educational flags, not treatment recommendations.
  • FDA-approved stem-cell products are limited. There is no FDA-approved injectable exosome product.
